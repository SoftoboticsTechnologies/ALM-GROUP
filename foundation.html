Empowering Lives. Strengthening Communities. Creating Opportunities.

Aarya Foundation is ALM Group’s associated social-welfare initiative, focused on healthcare, education, and employment for underserved communities.

Overview

Better access. Real opportunity.

Aarya Foundation is focused on supporting meaningful social development and improving access to opportunities for underserved communities.

The Foundation’s mission centres on healthcare, education, and employment. We believe social progress becomes more meaningful when people have better access to essential services, opportunities to learn, and pathways toward economic independence.

Mission

Promoting access and capability

To support social welfare by promoting access to healthcare, educational opportunities, skill development, and employment-related initiatives, with a focus on improving the well-being and capabilities of individuals and communities.

Vision

Dignified means of livelihood

To contribute to a society in which individuals have greater access to essential healthcare, quality learning opportunities, practical skills, and dignified means of livelihood.

Focus Areas

Healthcare. Education. Employment.

Three programme pillars that together strengthen individuals, families and communities.

Healthcare & Medical Welfare

Supporting initiatives that improve healthcare awareness and access to essential medical services, particularly for individuals and communities facing social or economic barriers.

Potential programmes

  • Community health-awareness campaigns
  • Medical-awareness and preventive-health initiatives
  • Health camps and medical outreach with qualified healthcare providers
  • Eligible medical-welfare programmes
  • Public education on hygiene, nutrition, and preventive care
  • Partnerships with hospitals, clinics, healthcare professionals, and eligible institutions

Education & Learning

Supporting educational initiatives that encourage learning, improve access to educational resources, and help individuals develop capabilities for the future.

Potential programmes

  • Educational support for disadvantaged students
  • Learning resources and educational materials
  • Scholarship and assistance programmes, subject to available resources and eligibility
  • Career guidance and educational counselling
  • Digital literacy and practical learning
  • Collaboration with schools, educational institutions, and community organizations

Employment, Skills & Livelihood

Exploring initiatives that help individuals improve employability, acquire relevant skills, and connect with suitable livelihood opportunities.

Potential programmes

  • Vocational and skill-development initiatives
  • Career guidance and employment awareness
  • Workplace-readiness training
  • Digital and professional skills development
  • Entrepreneurship awareness and livelihood support
  • Collaboration with training institutions, employers, and community partners
Social Impact Philosophy

Compassion, dignity and accountability

We believe social welfare should be guided by compassion, dignity, accountability, and a genuine understanding of community needs.

Respect for individual dignity

Responsible use of resources

Collaboration with qualified institutions and professionals

Clear programme objectives and documentation

Evaluation of programme outcomes

Inclusive access subject to programme criteria

Partner With the Foundation

When opportunities reach more people, communities grow stronger.

We welcome opportunities to explore collaboration with eligible organizations, healthcare providers, educational institutions, skill-development partners, volunteers, and socially responsible businesses. Together, we can work toward initiatives that improve access to healthcare, strengthen educational opportunities, and support employment and livelihood development.
