251-Acre Integrated Township

Residential • Commercial • Institutional • Equestrian • Lifestyle — a self-contained township destination by ALM Groups.

Executive Summary

A self-contained township destination

A planned, gated, mixed-use development bringing residential, commercial, institutional, sports, equestrian and lifestyle zones together across 251 acres.

  • Project: ALM Integrated Township
  • Developer: ALM Groups
  • Format: Planned, gated, mixed-use development
  • Land conversion: 1 acre = 40,000 sq.ft. (project-specific)

Equestrian area to be confirmed in the final DPR (brochure: 55 acres; master plan: 20 acres) and not double-counted in any other land-use category.
