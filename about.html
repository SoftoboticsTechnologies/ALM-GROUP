A Group Built Around Opportunity, Innovation and Growth

ALM Group is a diversified business group established with the ambition of developing and expanding business interests across multiple sectors.

Overview

Strategic Direction

The Group’s strategic direction encompasses real estate, air cargo transportation, passenger aviation, private airport infrastructure, and sustainable aviation fuel production.

Through specialized business verticals and future subsidiaries, ALM Group aims to build a portfolio of businesses capable of responding to changing market needs and emerging opportunities.

Operating Philosophy

We recognize that every industry requires specialized expertise, appropriate operating structures, sector-specific investment strategies, and compliance with applicable regulations. Accordingly, our long-term approach is to develop each business vertical with suitable professional capabilities, strategic partners, technical expertise, and responsible governance.

Corporate Structure

One overarching group brand

ALM Group serves as the overarching group brand for its business interests and planned portfolio of specialized ventures. As the Group expands, individual companies or subsidiaries may be established to focus on specific industries, manage sector-specific operations, pursue partnerships, and undertake relevant commercial activities.

The precise legal relationship between ALM Group, its operating companies, and Aarya Foundation will be described in accordance with the applicable corporate and registration documents.

Structure Benefits

  • Distinct identities for individual businesses
  • Specialized management and operational accountability
  • Flexible investment and partnership structures
  • Appropriate financial and legal separation between entities
  • Expansion into new sectors as business opportunities develop
