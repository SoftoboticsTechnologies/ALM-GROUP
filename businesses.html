Development Approach

Successful real estate development requires more than land acquisition. It involves location assessment, market research, financial planning, infrastructure requirements, development permissions, and a clear understanding of customer needs. ALM Group evaluates each opportunity according to its commercial feasibility, location, development potential, and applicable legal requirements, working with suitable partners and qualified professionals.

02 — Business Vertical

Air Cargo & Logistics

Connecting Businesses Through Air Cargo

ALM Group aims to develop opportunities in air cargo transportation and logistics, serving the evolving needs of domestic and, where feasible, international markets. Air cargo plays an important role in modern supply chains by facilitating transportation of time-sensitive shipments, commercial products, industrial components, valuable goods, and other suitable cargo.

Key Focus Areas

  • Domestic air cargo
  • Regional cargo connectivity
  • B2B cargo solutions
  • Time-sensitive shipments
  • Freight forwarder partnerships
  • Cargo handling & logistics
  • International operations (subject to permissions)

Development Approach

The development of our air cargo vertical may involve aircraft selection, route feasibility studies, cargo demand assessments, operating-cost analysis, airport partnerships, ground-handling arrangements, and regulatory approvals.

Balanced Business Models

We intend to evaluate business models that balance:

  • Payload capacity and route distance
  • Aircraft utilization
  • Operating expenses
  • Customer requirements
  • Commercial sustainability

Long-Term Objective

To develop an air cargo business capable of serving commercial customers through dependable planning, appropriate operating standards, and strategic industry relationships.

Exploring air cargo solutions that connect businesses, markets, and supply chains more efficiently.

03 — Business Vertical

Passenger Aviation

Connecting People. Opening New Possibilities.

ALM Group intends to explore opportunities in passenger air transportation, with a long-term focus on developing commercially viable air services that connect destinations and respond to passenger demand. Regional aviation can help improve connectivity between cities, business centres, emerging destinations, and areas where surface transportation may involve considerable travel time.

Key Focus Areas

  • Regional passenger transport
  • Domestic air connectivity
  • Short-haul route development
  • Aircraft acquisition & leasing
  • Operating & strategic partnerships
  • Route planning & demand assessment
  • Passenger service development

Connectivity

Evaluating routes that address genuine transportation needs.

Operational Efficiency

Assessing aircraft suitability, route structures, utilization, and operating costs.

Passenger Experience

Working toward reliable service, clear communication, and professional customer care.

Commercial Sustainability

Building business plans around realistic demand, appropriate pricing, and responsible financial management.

Our Objective

To develop the foundations for a passenger aviation business that connects destinations and creates new opportunities for travel and commerce. Potential expansion into additional markets remains subject to regulatory and commercial feasibility.

Regulatory Note: The launch and operation of passenger air services would be subject to applicable airline licensing, safety oversight, operational readiness, aircraft requirements, and other statutory approvals.

Connecting destinations and creating new opportunities for travel and commerce.

Private Airport Development & Aviation Infrastructure

Developing Infrastructure for the Future of Aviation

ALM Group aims to explore private airport development and associated aviation infrastructure opportunities to support the evolving needs of air transportation, cargo operations, business aviation, and regional connectivity.

Key Focus Areas

  • Private airport development
  • Aviation infrastructure planning
  • Cargo-oriented airport facilities
  • Regional aviation infrastructure
  • Business aviation facilities
  • Runway & airside planning
  • Airport access & ground support
  • Infrastructure partnerships

Development Principles

Airport projects require detailed feasibility studies, suitable land and airspace assessments, aviation safety evaluations, environmental review, infrastructure planning, and approvals from relevant authorities.

Individual Project Evaluation

Each proposed project must be evaluated individually according to its:

  • Location
  • Intended use
  • Technical specifications
  • Commercial viability
  • Applicable legal framework

Our Objective

To explore aviation infrastructure opportunities that support safe, efficient, and commercially viable air transportation, in partnership with infrastructure investors, engineering consultants, and aviation stakeholders.

Infrastructure that supports safe, efficient, and commercially viable air transportation.

05 — Business Vertical

Sustainable Aviation Fuel & Clean Energy

Exploring the Future of More Sustainable Aviation

As the aviation industry explores pathways to reduce lifecycle greenhouse gas emissions, sustainable aviation fuel has become an important area of technological development and investment interest. ALM Group intends to explore opportunities in sustainable aviation fuel production and related clean-energy value chains.

Key Focus Areas

  • SAF production
  • Feedstock & supply chain
  • Technology & process evaluation
  • Production facility development
  • Technology partnerships
  • Fuel quality & certification
  • Low-carbon energy opportunities

Development Approach

A sustainable aviation fuel facility requires technical and commercial assessment, including:

  • Feedstock sourcing and process selection
  • Production economics
  • Environmental performance
  • Infrastructure requirements
  • Product certification and market demand

Our Priorities

  • Technical feasibility
  • Responsible resource management
  • Credible lifecycle-emissions assessment
  • Compliance with relevant fuel specifications and environmental regulations

Our Objective

To explore commercially viable opportunities in sustainable aviation fuel and contribute to the development of a more sustainable aviation ecosystem.

Contributing to the development of a more sustainable aviation ecosystem.
